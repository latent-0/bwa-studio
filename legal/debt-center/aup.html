This AUP is designed to:

Protect the legal rights, security, and reputation of Debt Center and all users;

Promote compliance with applicable laws and regulations; and

Ensure the integrity, reliability, and quality of the Services across all Debt Center brands.

2. General Rules of Conduct

You must not (and must not allow, encourage, or enable any third party to):

Illegal Activity

Use the services for illegal, harmful, or fraudulent activities (e.g., money laundering, terrorism, sale of illicit goods).

Unlawful Marketing

Violate the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA), CAN-SPAM Act, or state "mini-TCPA" laws.

Disregard Do-Not-Call (DNC) rules.

Send unsolicited or unconsented marketing communications.

Spoof caller ID.

Privacy Violations

Collect, share, or resell personal data without proper legal consent.

Disregard opt-out signals (e.g., Global Privacy Control).

Ignore data-subject rights.

Harassment & Abuse

Transmit content that is harassing, defamatory, threatening, hateful, or discriminatory.

Intellectual-Property Infringement

Upload/distribute content that infringes on copyright, trademark, or other IP rights.

Violate the DMCA.

Security Violations

Introduce malware, viruses, or harmful code.

Attempt unauthorized access.

Scan/probe/test for vulnerabilities.

Conduct denial-of-service attacks.

Automated Scraping

Scrape, crawl, spider, or harvest data from the site/services without written permission.

Misrepresentation

Impersonate any person or entity.

Misstate or forge headers, email sender info, or caller ID information.

Resale Without Compliance

Resell data, leads, or calls to third parties who don’t meet the required legal and contractual standards.

Excessive Load

Take any action that creates an unreasonable or disproportionately large load on the infrastructure.

You must:

To report a violation of this AUP, please email aup@debt.center with the subject line “AUP Violation” and include:

We will investigate and, if we deem necessary, take appropriate action.
