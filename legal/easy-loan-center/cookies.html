2.3 “Personal Information” or “Personal Data” means information that identifies, relates to, describes, is reasonably capable of being associated with, or could reasonably be linked to an identified or identifiable individual, household, browser, or device, as defined under applicable law.

2.4 “Targeted Advertising” means advertising selected based on Personal Information obtained from a visitor’s activities over time and across nonaffiliated websites, applications, services, or devices, as defined under applicable law. The term may not include contextual advertising based solely on the current interaction with the Site.

4. Types and Duration of Cookies

4.1 First-Party and Third-Party Cookies

First-party cookies are set or controlled by the Site a visitor is using. Third-party cookies are set or controlled by an organization other than the operator of the Site, such as an analytics, advertising, affiliate, video, form, call-tracking, hosting, security, or lead-services provider.

4.2 Session and Persistent Cookies

Session cookies generally expire when the browser session ends. Persistent cookies remain until their stated expiration, until they are removed by the visitor, or until they are deleted or replaced by the organization that set them.

4.3 Duration

Cookie duration varies by purpose, provider, and configuration. Where available, information about individual cookies and storage technologies, including their categories, purposes, domains, and duration, is provided through the cookie consent interface when displayed. Information already collected can be retained separately from a cookie, as described in Section 18.
